1Gov Mail · Executive summary · September 2026

Inbox Intelligence Brief

1Gov Mail now includes a full AI layer that helps staff write faster, read less, and miss nothing — built entirely on government-hosted infrastructure. No email content ever leaves the RISA network, and no AI output is ever sent or acted on without a person approving it.

Sovereign by design
All AI runs on our own servers. No foreign cloud AI services, no data sharing, no per-use fees.
Works in three languages
English, French, and Kinyarwanda — the AI answers, summarizes, and searches in the language of the email.
People stay in control
The AI drafts, flags, and suggests. A person always reviews before anything is sent or closed.

What staff can do today

Seven capabilities, in the order a working day meets them.

Executive Briefingone click, every morning
A single card summarizing what needs attention right now: requests directed at you, stated deadlines, and what you're waiting on from others.
Triage labelsautomatic, in the inbox
Every recent email is automatically tagged Needs decision, Waiting on you, Deadline, or FYI — the inbox sorts itself by urgency instead of arrival time.
Commitment trackerrunning ledger
Keeps a list of what you promised others and what others promised you, drawn from real correspondence. It suggests when something looks settled — it never closes an item on its own.
Ask Your InboxIn progressquestion in, answer out
Ask a plain question — "what did finance approve last month?" — and get an answer built only from your own mail, with every claim linked to the email it came from.
Smarter searchIn progressmeaning, not just keywords
Search finds emails that match what you mean, not only the exact words you typed — across all three languages.
Summarizelong emails & threads
Condenses a long email or a whole back-and-forth thread into a few sentences, in the message's own language.
Writing helprewrite & suggest reply
Polishes a draft — formal for official correspondence, shorter, friendlier, or grammar-checked — or proposes a reply (accept, decline, acknowledge, request details) that the sender reviews and edits before sending.
Status All seven capabilities are built, reviewed, and fully tested. Five are ready for the pilot; Ask Your Inbox and Smarter search are marked in progress while their language-understanding model (bge-m3) is installed on the AI server — the final step before the staff pilot.
